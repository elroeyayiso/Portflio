DESIGN SPACE

Understanding the journey.

Initially, my research topic encompassed transportation as a whole, but I subsequently narrowed it down to specifically focus on public transportation and the waiting times experienced by commuters. I wanted to understand how waiting times for public transportation impact a person's daily routine.

I arrived at this topic because public transportation is easily noticeable in Addis Ababa. It can be seen in the faces of commuters waiting at stations and in the ever-moving traffic of city buses.

My exploration of the public transportation design space is motivated by a desire to unravel the difficulty of movement within cities.

The Addis Ababa public transportation system is home to a diverse range of actors. From commuters relying on these systems to reach their workplaces or homes to the transportation authorities and urban planners responsible for their operation and development, the ecosystem is complex.

By exploring the experiences, challenges, and interactions of those involved, I aimed to contribute to a richer understanding of how public transportation shapes Addis Ababa and influences the lives of its residents.

DATA COLLECTION

Listening to the people in the system.

In Addis Ababa, where the public transportation system faces challenges with long waiting times for commuters, an opportunity to optimize services was identified through data collection methods, including surveys and interviews, although some difficulties were encountered, such as reluctance and incomplete survey responses.

This feedback provided valuable insights into accessibility, affordability, and the waiting experience related to public transportation.

Furthermore, I've incorporated pie charts to visually illustrate the overall percentages for each question in the survey.

I chose to gather feedback from stakeholders to assess their perception of the proposed app.

I also interviewed the director for Sheger and Anbessa City Bus Enterprise, during the interview he mentioned Addis Ababa faces a multifaceted set of challenges in its public transportation system.

Firstly, the city is experiencing rapid population growth driven by factors such as job opportunities and regional conflicts, resulting in an increased demand for public transportation services.

However, technical issues with the city's buses pose a significant problem. Maintenance problems within the bus fleet lead to service interruptions, reduced reliability, frequent breakdowns, and subsequent commuter dissatisfaction.

These technical issues not only affect passenger satisfaction but also result in a loss of revenue for the transportation department, as commuters seek alternative transportation modes.

Moreover, declining trust in the reliability of bus services causes passengers to opt for other means of transport.

Finally, funding constraints have emerged as a substantial barrier, limiting the department's ability to address these technical challenges and invest in modernizing the transportation infrastructure.

In light of these challenges, there is a pressing need for investment in public transportation to accommodate the growing population and improve the overall reliability and efficiency of the system.

04
STAKEHOLDERS

A system made up of people.

Various stakeholders are involved in improving Addis Ababa's public transportation system. Commuters benefit from enhanced accessibility, affordability, and reduced waiting times.

Government authorities, such as the Addis Ababa Transport Authority, are pivotal in planning and implementing changes.

Public transportation operators, including Anbessa City Bus Enterprise, provide the services and can introduce technological solutions.

Urban planners and architects contribute to infrastructure design.

Community organizations advocate for specific communities, while academic institutions offer research and data for optimization.

Finally, media and public opinion can raise awareness and garner support for transportation improvements.

These diverse stakeholders collectively influence the development and enhancement of the city's public transportation system.

INTERVENTION NARRATIVE

A possible solution: real-time information.

To address the waiting time issue and enhance the public transportation system, a proposed intervention narrative focuses on the development of a Real-Time Bus Tracking Mobile App.

This initiative would involve collaboration among various stakeholders, including Technology Companies, Addis Ababa Transport Authority, Transportation Operators, Urban Planners, Community Organizations, Academic Institutions, and Media.

Government authorities will facilitate planning and implementation of transportation improvements.

07
LIMITATIONS / NEXT STEPS

What remains to be solved.

In my pursuit of understanding public transportation in Addis Ababa, several limitations have emerged.

Communication barriers pose a challenge, making it difficult to coordinate efforts effectively among different stakeholders.

Additionally, finding individuals willing to share personal information for data collection purposes can be a hurdle, as privacy concerns often come into play.

Furthermore, encountering challenges with the Transportation Office has added complexity to the project.

Lastly, managing survey lines during peak hours can be logistically challenging due to the high volume of commuters.

01

Gather more data

Gathering more data is essential to inform decision-making and address the identified limitations.

02

Engage commuters

This will involve engaging with commuters and collecting data responsibly.

03

Work with authorities

Communicating with transportation authorities is also a priority to ensure alignment and collaboration with government policies and funding.

04

Design the platform

Designing an app or creating a simplified platform can enhance communication and data collection while streamlining efforts among stakeholders.

These steps will help overcome challenges and pave the way for a more efficient and user-friendly public transportation system in Addis Ababa.
